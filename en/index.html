Who I am & Why I build

Hi, I'm Cynthia. Originally from eastern France but a nomad at heart, I've lived across France, Japan, the US, China, and South Korea.

With a dual background in engineering and business, paired with an artist's soul, I now specialize in language learning methodology. I design educational experiences that weave together pedagogy, creativity, and technology.

I'm deeply fascinated by what makes language acquisition effective, and specifically how digital tools can elevate that journey.

This space is my "open studio" for that exploration. Here, I share worksheets, pedagogical videos, and songs, to personal reflections, publications, and my latest experiments with AI and language apps.
